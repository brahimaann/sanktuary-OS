# Sanktuary

**Music, community and the Twin Cities arts scene.** Visit at **[sanktuary.studio](https://sanktuary.studio)**.

Sanktuary is a creative hub for the Twin Cities. It brings together the artists we host, the stories of the local
scene, and the clothes we make, all under one roof.

## What's inside

### MRND: music and talent

The talent we host and the music we make together: artists, releases and where to listen to them.

### MHOTC: community

The people, places and nights of the Twin Cities arts scene, with events, stories and vlogs. MHOTC is home to
**CITIES**, our journal of the local arts scene ([sanktuary.studio/cities](https://sanktuary.studio/cities)):

- **Heart of the Cities**: profiles, case studies and spotlights.
- **Essays**: essays, manifestos and our Substack.
- **Field Notes**: straight from the field, from shoots, sessions and shows.
- **Videos**: vlogs and videos from Twin Cities creators.

Want to be featured or write for CITIES? Start a draft at [sanktuary.studio/write](https://sanktuary.studio/write).

### Shop

Apparel and releases, including SOTG and Merakaiya, at [sanktuary.studio/shop](https://sanktuary.studio/shop).

### The Sanktuary desktop

Behind the public site, members work in a Windows 98-style desktop: shared team spaces for files, live moodboards,
planners, chat and more. Membership is by invitation.

## Built with

React, TypeScript and Vite on the front end, a small Node.js server behind it, and [Clerk](https://clerk.com) for
member sign-in.
